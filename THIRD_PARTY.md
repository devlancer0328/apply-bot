# Third-party libraries

Runtime dependencies are bundled locally. Their license texts are included under `src/vendor/`.

- SQL.js: MIT; SQLite itself is in the public domain.
- pdf-lib: MIT.
- PDF.js (`pdfjs-dist`): Apache License 2.0.
- Zod: MIT.

Development dependencies, including esbuild, LinkeDOM and Playwright, are declared in `package.json` and pinned through `package-lock.json`.
